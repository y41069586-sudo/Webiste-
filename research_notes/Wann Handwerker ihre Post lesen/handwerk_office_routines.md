# Büroarbeit und Post in kleinen Handwerksbetrieben: wann, wer, wie lange

Context: advertising letters with a QR code to a demo website, delivered 12–16 Nov 2026 to 58 small craft businesses (Elektro, SHK, Maler, Tischler, Dachdecker, GaLaBau, Glaser), Dortmund area. Research date: 13 Nov 2026. Around 19 tool calls. Source types are marked: [Verband/Kammer] = trade association or chamber, [Institut] = research institute, [Fachpresse] = trade press, [Anbieter] = vendor with commercial interest, [Ratgeber] = blog/advice content without data.

**Overall caveat:** I found **no survey that directly measures when Handwerk owners open postal mail, or which weekday/time they do office work.** Everything on timing ("evenings", "Friday") is qualitative or advisory. The quantitative evidence covers *how much* office/bureaucracy time there is and *who* does it, not *when*.

## 1. How much office work, and when (evening/weekend/Bürotag)?

### Takeaway
Office and bureaucracy work is substantial: about 5+ extra hours per week for every second business in the HWK Dortmund district, and 42 % of plancraft respondents (vendor) spend more than half their time on office work. The "evening after the job site" pattern is widely described, but only in vendor and advice texts, not in measured survey data. No source quantifies weekend or fixed-Bürotag shares.

### Cited Findings
- **HWK Dortmund special survey (Oct 2023), [Kammer], directly relevant region:** about 700 business owners surveyed alongside the Konjunkturumfrage. "Jeder zweite Betrieb im Kammerbezirk Dortmund verbringt mittlerweile mehr als fünf Stunden zusätzlich pro Woche damit, bürokratische Aufgaben zu erledigen." 89 % say the effort rose over five years. 61 % say this leaves less time for orders, and 17 % avoid hiring to limit the bureaucratic effort. No data on time of day. — [HWK Dortmund, Presseinformation 57/2023](https://www.hwk-do.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/HWK-Pressedienst-57-Konjunktur-Sonderumfrage_Buerokratiebelastungen-im-Handwerk.pdf)
- **ZDH Sonderumfrage Bürokratiebelastung (Q1 2023), [Verband]:** 10,630 businesses, weighted by employee size. 74 % say bureaucracy increased in five years, 76 % name constant legal changes as the biggest burden, 54 % name documentation duties, and 58 % say self-employment is becoming less attractive. The landing page gives no hours or timing data. — [ZDH](https://www.zdh.de/ueber-uns/fachbereich-wirtschaft-energie-umwelt/sonderumfragen/sonderumfrage-buerokratiebelastung-im-handwerk/)
- An older chamber survey (HWK Dortmund "Sonderumfrage zum Bürokratieabbau Frühjahr 2020") is reported in search summaries as giving "knapp 13 Stunden pro Woche" for bureaucracy and documentation. **I did not verify this in the PDF**, so treat it as unconfirmed. — [HWK Dortmund 2020 PDF](https://www.hwk-do.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Sonderumfrage-zum-Buerokratieabbau-Fruehjahr-2020.pdf)
- **plancraft survey (reported 14 Aug 2026), [Anbieter], craft software vendor:** 1,026 businesses in DE/AT. 42 % spend more than half of their working time on Büroarbeit rather than on site. The biggest time sinks are e-mails, quotes and invoices. Three quarters say AI saves them 1–5 h of office work per week. No data on time of day. — [Handwerksblatt](https://www.handwerksblatt.de/betriebsfuehrung/umfrage-von-plancraft-jeder-dritte-handwerker-nutzt-bereits-taeglich-ki)
- **"Evening" pattern, qualitative only, [Anbieter]:** "abends sitzt Du dann noch im Büro, um Rechnungen zu schreiben, Belege zu sortieren und Steuern vorzubereiten … bleibt für die Buchhaltung oft kaum Zeit übrig" (Papierkram, accounting software vendor, 29 Dec 2025). It gives no figures. — [Papierkram](https://www.papierkram.de/aktuelles/buchhaltungssoftware-handwerker/)
- **Long days, [Fachpresse/Interview, 2019]:** An efficiency trainer describes the "zwölf- oder 14-Stunden-Tag" as common among self-employed Handwerker and gives the example of a 60-hour week. This is an expert opinion, not data. — [Handwerksblatt, Zeitmanagement für Chefs im Handwerk (13.07.2019)](https://www.handwerksblatt.de/betriebsfuehrung/zeitmanagement-fuer-chefs-im-handwerk)
- **Fixed office day, advice only, [Fachpresse]:** A Handwerksblatt article on office organisation reportedly recommends fixed slots, e.g. writing quotes and invoices "immer freitags" and making customer visits Tuesday/Wednesday. This comes from the search-result summary and was **not verified by fetch**. It is a recommendation, not observed behaviour. — [Handwerksblatt, Büroorganisation ist Chefsache](https://www.handwerksblatt.de/themen-specials/was-sie-als-chef-im-handwerk-wissen-muessen/bueroorganisation-ist-chefsache)
- **DHZ, [Fachpresse]:** Invoicing, payroll and tax count for many Handwerker as tedious, time-consuming duties in an already full working day (outsourcing article, from search summary). — [Deutsche Handwerks Zeitung](https://www.deutsche-handwerks-zeitung.de/?p=160281)
- **General working hours:** A secondary source cites Destatis (2024): 44.5 % of self-employed people with employees regularly work 49+ hours per week. This is not Handwerk-specific, and I did not verify it at Destatis. — [heylead.de (Ratgeber, cites Destatis)](https://www.heylead.de/ratgeber/stress-handwerksbetrieb-inhaber)

### Inferences
- For owner-run firms without office staff, office time most plausibly happens **after site work (roughly 17–21 h) and partly on Saturday**. This fits the "abends im Büro" narrative and the long-day evidence. It is an inference from qualitative sources, not measured.
- Because the bureaucracy load has grown (HWK Dortmund: 89 % say rising), unsolicited post competes with a backlog of mandatory paperwork. That raises the chance that advertising mail is batched and handled later rather than on the day of delivery.

### Gaps
- No survey (ZDH, HWK, Bitkom, KfW, UFH or vendors such as ToolTime, HERO, Craftboxx, Lexware, sevDesk) was found that reports the **share of office work done in the evening or at the weekend**, or the prevalence of a fixed Bürotag. Searches for vendor surveys on "Büroarbeit abends/Wochenende" returned nothing quantitative.
- The full ZDH 2023 Ergebnisbericht (PDF) was not read and may contain hours by company size.

## 2. Who handles post and office work, and how is advertising mail treated?

### Takeaway
In family-run craft firms, the owner's partner (usually the wife) typically runs the office: bookkeeping, office organisation and customer care, around 8–10 h/week each, per the itb/UFH study. UFH claims about 75 % of craft firms are run jointly by a couple. In those firms, the partner is the likely first reader of incoming post. There is no data on how advertising post specifically is sorted.

### Cited Findings
- **itb im DHI study, commissioned by UFH (published 2018, DHI programme 2016/2017), [Institut, association-commissioned]:** "Die Bedeutung mitarbeitender Unternehmerfrauen für ein zukunftsfähiges Handwerk" (Bauer, Schliephake, Hauser). It surveyed roughly n≈320 women. Many were UFH members (65 % volunteer in the UFH), so the sample is **biased toward organised, engaged Unternehmerfrauen**. Average hours per week by task: Buchhaltung/Finanzen 10.2 h, **Büroorganisation 8.6 h**, Betriebsführung 8 h, Kundenpflege 7.7 h, Verkauf 7.4 h, EDV/IT 6.8 h, Marketing 5.9 h, handwerkliche Tätigkeiten 2.2 h. 65 % are mitarbeitende (Ehe-)Partnerinnen, 43 % co-founded the firm, and 71 % say the business would not run without them. 46 % are in the Ausbaugewerbe, a third of those in SHK. Outside the firm they spend 13.8 h/week on household and 7.3 h on childcare, which suggests office work is fitted around family time. — [itb PDF](https://www.itb.de/download/die-bedeutung-mitarbeitender-unternehmerfrauen-fuer-ein-zukunftsfaehiges-handwerk); summary: [DHZ "Unternehmerfrau 2018"](https://www.deutsche-handwerks-zeitung.de/?p=93027)
- **Qualitative interviews in the same study:** "Meist übernehme die Unternehmerfrau den kaufmännischen Bereich", meaning office organisation, finance, investments, staff and partly marketing. The husband covers execution, planning and technical matters. — [itb PDF, p. 56](https://www.itb.de/download/die-bedeutung-mitarbeitender-unternehmerfrauen-fuer-ein-zukunftsfaehiges-handwerk)
- **UFH via DHZ (6 Jul 2026), [Verband/Fachpresse]:** "rund 75 Prozent aller Handwerksunternehmen" are run as family businesses jointly by husband and wife. The Unternehmerfrau "übernimmt in der Regel das Backoffice, das Personal und das Büro". No survey year or method is given, so this is an association claim. — [DHZ, Frauen im Handwerk: Die Zahlen im Check](https://www.deutsche-handwerks-zeitung.de/?p=380502)
- **Older association data (1999), [Verband, outdated]:** In about 70 % of businesses, the master's wife was responsible for the commercial area. — [SBZ 24/1999 PDF](https://www.sbz-online.de/sites/default/files/ulmer/de-sbz/document/file_188851.pdf) (from search summary, not verified in full)
- **Small firms (1–5 people), [Anbieter/Ratgeber]:** The owner typically takes on many tasks simultaneously and delegates little. — [Streit Software, Organisation im Handwerksbetrieb](https://www.streit-software.de/wissen/organisation-im-handwerksbetrieb)
- **Treatment of mail, B2B in general, [Anbieter: Deutsche Post]:** Physical mailings are described qualitatively as staying on the desk, going into a drawer, being taken out again and being passed to colleagues. No retention figures are given. — [Deutsche Post blog, B2B-Marketing Print-Mailings](https://www.deutschepost.de/de/p/print-mailing/insights/blog/b2b-marketing-print-mailings.html)
- **Response lag, [Anbieter: CMC/Deutsche Post, mostly e-commerce, not Handwerk]:** "47 Prozent aller Bestellungen erst ab der fünften Woche nach Versand". The study's own B2B results are flagged as non-representative because of low case numbers. — [Deutsche Post blog](https://www.deutschepost.de/de/p/print-mailing/insights/blog/b2b-marketing-print-mailings.html); [DHL Group PM CMC 2025](https://group.dhl.com/de/presse/pressemitteilungen/2025/cmc-print-mailing-studie-2025.html)
- **Mailbox emptying, consumers, [Anbieter: Deutsche Post Dialogmarketing Monitor 2026]:** 74 % empty their mailbox at least five times per week, and 87 % of those open to postal advertising open addressed advertising mail (from search summaries). These are consumer figures, not business figures. — [Horizont, Dialogmarketing Monitor 2026](https://www.horizont.net/marketing/nachrichten/dialogmarketing-monitor-2026-sparen-ja-beeinflussung-nein-was-konsumentinnen-heute-wirklich-von-werbung-erwarten-236052)
- **Handwerk-specific advice on letters, [Fachpresse]:** A marketing expert in Handwerksblatt says recipients quickly recognise Dialogpost as mass mail. A fully stamped letter often arrives the next day, while Dialogpost can sit for days in the sorting centre (from search summary). — [Handwerksblatt, Die Renaissance des Werbebriefs](https://www.handwerksblatt.de/betriebsfuehrung/die-renaissance-des-werbebriefs)

### Inferences
- **Two reader types among the 58 businesses:**
  - **(a) With a partner or office help.** Post is likely opened during daytime office hours on the day of delivery or the next working day. The first reader is the partner, who then decides whether to show it to the owner, probably in the evening. That is a two-step path, so QR scans may lag by one or more days and may happen in the evening when the owner looks.
  - **(b) Solo or owner-only firms.** Post likely sits until the evening or a weekend office slot. Scans would cluster on weekday evenings and Saturday/Sunday.
- **Advertising mail is likely triaged.** Invoices, authority letters and supplier mail come first, and advertising is set aside or put on a pile. The Deutsche Post "stays on desk/drawer" description and the CMC "47 % after week 5" figure suggest a long tail of responses. That tail is weeks, not days, but the CMC figure is e-commerce reorder behaviour and only weakly transferable.
- For letters delivered 12–16 Nov 2026 (Thu–Mon), plausible first-look windows would be the same or next weekday (office person), the evenings of 12–20 Nov, and the weekends of 14–15 and 21–22 Nov. This is an inference, not evidence.

### Gaps
- No German study found on **how long post sits before being read in small businesses**, or on the share of advertising mail discarded unopened in Handwerk firms.
- No data on the share of solo businesses among the target trades (the Handwerkszählung or Destatis could provide this; not searched).
- The UFH "75 %" figure has no stated method or year. The itb sample is UFH-member-heavy and likely overstates the professional office role of partners in very small firms.

## 3. Seasonal workload in November/December

### Takeaway
I found no direct evidence on November/December office time in German Handwerk. Konjunktur data show order backlogs of roughly 9 weeks (Bremen, spring 2026). Year-end peaks (finishing jobs before winter or Christmas, year-end invoicing, Jahresabschluss preparation) are plausible but undocumented in the sources found.

### Cited Findings
- **HWK Bremen Konjunkturbericht Frühjahr 2026, [Kammer]:** average order reach of 8.8 weeks (Herbst 2025: 8.9 weeks). Business climate index 98 (Herbst 2025: 100). — [HWK Bremen PDF (search summary)](https://www.hwk-bremen.de/_Resources/Persistent/f/8/d/1/f8d110c0f2098a828aed20a5a0ec595f5e468440/2026.04.09_Konjunkturbericht%20Fr%C3%BChjahr%202026_final.pdf)
- **ZDH, early 2026, [Verband]:** businesses are holding back on investment until the economy stabilises (search summary). — [ZDH statement](https://www.zdh.de/presse/veroeffentlichungen/interviews-und-statements/handwerkskonjunkturmotor-springt-nicht-an/)
- **DHZ, [Fachpresse]:** "Handwerkskonjunktur: Stabile Stimmung, schleppende Geschäfte", with construction as the weak spot (headline only). — [DHZ](https://www.deutsche-handwerks-zeitung.de/handwerkskonjunktur-stabile-stimmung-schleppende-geschaefte-386089/)

### Inferences
- A full order book of about 2 months, plus outdoor trades (Dachdecker, GaLaBau, Maler exterior) pushing to finish before frost, likely means long site days in November. That leaves office time even more compressed into evenings and weekends.
- GaLaBau and exterior painting may have *more* slack from late November, which could mean more time to look at mail.
- With subdued construction demand in 2026, some firms may be more receptive to marketing offers. This is speculative.

### Gaps
- No ZDH or HWK Q3/Herbst 2026 Konjunktur data for NRW or Dortmund was retrieved.
- No source documents a year-end office crunch (Jahresabschluss, year-end invoicing) for small Handwerk firms.

## 4. When do Handwerker check digital things (smartphone, evenings)?

### Takeaway
I found no Handwerk-specific data on the times of day for smartphone or e-mail use. Bitkom/ZDH 2020 shows strong pressure for fast responses. General Bitkom data show widespread after-hours availability among employees.

### Cited Findings
- **Bitkom/ZDH "So digital ist das Handwerk 2020", [Verband + Bitkom Research]:** about 502 craft businesses surveyed Jan/Feb 2020. 97 % say customers expect fast responses because of digitalisation, and 64 % call digitalisation a challenge (search summaries). — [Bitkom/ZDH press release PDF](https://www.handwerkdigital.de/deulocal/textbilder/images/Pressemitteilungen/200304_Presseinfo_DigitalesHandwerk2020.pdf); [DHZ](https://www.deutsche-handwerks-zeitung.de/?p=250765)
- **plancraft 2026, [Anbieter]:** 70 % of businesses use AI and a third use it daily. Communication is the top use (48 %), and e-mails are among the biggest time sinks. — [Handwerksblatt](https://www.handwerksblatt.de/betriebsfuehrung/umfrage-von-plancraft-jeder-dritte-handwerker-nutzt-bereits-taeglich-ki)
- **Bitkom (older, all employees, not Handwerk):** 29 % of employees are reachable by phone or e-mail for work at any time. — [winfuture.de](https://winfuture.de/news,64116.html)

### Inferences
- Scanning a QR code requires the smartphone in hand while looking at the letter. Scans are therefore likely when the letter is physically in front of the owner: at the office desk in the evening or weekend, or when the partner hands it over. Scans during site hours are less likely.

### Gaps
- No time-of-day usage data for Handwerk owners. The project's own scan-event timestamps from these 58 letters would be the best evidence available.
