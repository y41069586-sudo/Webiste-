# Company legitimacy: SIECOM IT-Systemhaus GmbH (easyNova / digiBrief)

All sources accessed 13.11.2026 unless noted otherwise. "Vendor claim" means the statement comes only from easyNova's or Siecom's own websites and was not independently confirmed.

## 1. Does SIECOM IT-Systemhaus GmbH exist in the Handelsregister (HRB 25046, AG Dresden)? Since when, managing directors, insolvency or liquidation, address changes?

### Takeaway
Yes. The company has been registered since 23.11.2006 under HRB 25046 at AG Dresden and has filed annual accounts every year from 2006 to 2024. The latest filing was published 27.05.2026. North Data shows no insolvency or liquidation notice. It is a long-standing small GmbH, not a newly founded shell company.

### Cited Findings
- Registered on 23.11.2006 as "Siecom Computer & Software GmbH" with HRB 25046 at Amtsgericht Dresden, share capital 25.000 €. Renamed "Siecom IT-Systemhaus GmbH" on 05.06.2008. EUID DEU1104.HRB25046, LEI 98450075EFD1E550FL86 — [North Data](https://www.northdata.de/SIECOM+IT-Systemhaus+GmbH,+Dresden/Amtsgericht+Dresden+HRB+25046)
- Current address in the register: Gutzkowstr. 30, 01069 Dresden. There was one address-change publication on 28.01.2010 (the previous address is not visible on the free page) — [North Data](https://www.northdata.de/SIECOM+IT-Systemhaus+GmbH,+Dresden/Amtsgericht+Dresden+HRB+25046)
- Managing director at registration (2006): Burkhard Fels. North Data currently shows one managing director, but the name is behind a paywall. The easyNova Impressum, last updated 01.10.2026, names Burkhard Fels as Geschäftsführer, so the person appears to be the same since 2006 — [North Data](https://www.northdata.de/SIECOM+IT-Systemhaus+GmbH,+Dresden/Amtsgericht+Dresden+HRB+25046); [easyNova Impressum](https://www.easynova.de/impressum)
- Two active shareholders (names behind a paywall). Shareholder lists were filed regularly, the latest on 16.06.2021 — [North Data](https://www.northdata.de/SIECOM+IT-Systemhaus+GmbH,+Dresden/Amtsgericht+Dresden+HRB+25046)
- Annual accounts were published for every financial year from 2006 to 2024. The FY2024 accounts were published 27.05.2026. The figures (balance-sheet total, equity, employees) are behind North Data's paywall — [North Data](https://www.northdata.de/SIECOM+IT-Systemhaus+GmbH,+Dresden/Amtsgericht+Dresden+HRB+25046)
- Mergers: Drecom Computer-Vertriebs GmbH, Dresden (HRB 3643) was merged in on 21.08.2017, and Fels Consulting UG, Moritzburg (HRB 36144) on 09.09.2021 — [North Data](https://www.northdata.de/SIECOM+IT-Systemhaus+GmbH,+Dresden/Amtsgericht+Dresden+HRB+25046)
- Word mark "Siecom" and word/figurative mark "SIECOM.de" (2011) — [North Data](https://www.northdata.de/SIECOM+IT-Systemhaus+GmbH,+Dresden/Amtsgericht+Dresden+HRB+25046)
- North Data's visible content shows no insolvency or liquidation notices. North Data itself notes that its pages are generated automatically and may contain errors — [North Data](https://www.northdata.de/SIECOM+IT-Systemhaus+GmbH,+Dresden/Amtsgericht+Dresden+HRB+25046)
- The Impressum matches the register: HRB 25046, AG Dresden, USt-IdNr. DE 250 271 663, phone +49 351 86675-600, info@easynova.de. Copyright "© 2007–2026 Siecom IT-Systemhaus GmbH" — [easyNova Impressum](https://www.easynova.de/impressum)
- An eBay seller account "siecom-de" with about 706 sold items lists HRB 25046 — [eBay seller profile](https://www.ebay.de/usr/siecom-de) (seen only in a search snippet, not opened)

### Inferences
- The Impressum data (name, HRB, court, address, managing director) agrees with the register data. That speaks against identity theft or a fake Impressum.
- Annual accounts filed without gaps for about 19 years, plus two absorbed group companies (including Drecom, an older Dresden computer dealer), describe a continuously operating small business. Fraudulent "fly-by-night" operators usually have young or recently bought shell GmbHs, frequent changes of managing director and missing annual filings. None of these warning signs is visible here.
- Siecom's claim "since 1991" (see section 2) most likely refers to the predecessor Drecom Computer-Vertriebs GmbH (low HRB number 3643, so an old registration). The GmbH that exists today dates from 2006. This is an inference and not confirmed by a source.

### Gaps
- I could not see the financial figures (balance-sheet total, equity, employees) or the shareholder names. They are behind the North Data paywall. A free look is possible on [unternehmensregister.de](https://www.unternehmensregister.de) or [bundesanzeiger.de](https://www.bundesanzeiger.de): search for "Siecom IT-Systemhaus", which shows the annual accounts, usually as a small-company balance sheet only.
- The current Handelsregister extract from handelsregister.de was not retrieved, and the openregister.de request returned 403. So the current managing director is confirmed only by the Impressum, not by an official extract.
- I did not directly check the official insolvency portal [insolvenzbekanntmachungen.de](https://neu.insolvenzbekanntmachungen.de). The statement "no insolvency" rests on North Data only.

## 2. How long has easyNova / digiBrief existed? Press, partnerships, certifications, references, customer reviews

### Takeaway
By its own account, easyNova has run its hybrid-mail platform since 2016. There is little independent evidence: no press coverage, only a few reviews, and all certificates and partnerships are vendor claims that I could not verify. This points to a small niche provider, not to fraud, but the claims should not be taken at face value.

### Cited Findings
- Vendor claim: "gegründet 2006 und seither am Standort Dresden ansässig". Also: "Die hybride Briefplattform betreiben wir seit 2016 für unsere Kunden", where easyNova started as "E-POST Premium Partner der Deutschen Post" — [easyNova Unternehmen](https://www.easynova.de/unternehmen/)
- Vendor claim: today it also works with private postal providers. Printing and enveloping are done by connected print service providers "ausschließlich an Standorten in Deutschland". The software is also offered as white label — [easyNova Unternehmen](https://www.easynova.de/unternehmen/)
- Vendor claim: delivery "durch die Deutsche Post" in 1–3 working days. Prices from 0,65 € net per standard letter, with no base fee or contract term (price table "Stand 01.01.2026") — [easyNova Startseite](https://www.easynova.de/); [digiBrief product page](https://www.easynova.de/produkte/digibrief/)
- Vendor claim: licensed postal service provider with the Bundesnetzagentur, licence "L 4436" (§ 6 PostG) — [easyNova Unternehmen](https://www.easynova.de/unternehmen/)
- Not verifiable: the Bundesnetzagentur provider directory page does not offer a downloadable list and does not mention Siecom, easyNova or L 4436. Since the new Postgesetz (July 2024), providers must be entered in the public Anbieterverzeichnis. Old notifications only remained valid until 18.08.2026 — [BNetzA Anbieterverzeichnis Post](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/post-anbieterverzeichnis); [BNetzA Post overview](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Fachthemen/Post/Anbieterverzeichnis/start.html)
- Vendor claim: ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 27001:2022, combined certificate "251016 08 IMS" from DICIS AG, issued 16.10.2025 and valid until 16.10.2028 — [easyNova Unternehmen](https://www.easynova.de/unternehmen/)
- Vendor claim: "Innovationspreis-IT 'BEST OF 2017'" in the category Office Management — [easyNova Startseite](https://www.easynova.de/). A search found no independent mention of easyNova in connection with this award — [search result set, Innovationspreis-IT 2017](https://www.deutscherpresseindex.de/?p=6439). This award is given by the Initiative Mittelstand. Besides category winners it names many "BEST OF" entries, which are published in a paid list — [Best-of-2012 list (example)](https://www.gbconsite.de:443/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/Best-of-2012-Innovationspreis-IT-Bestenliste.pdf); [Händlerbund: entry free of charge](https://ohn.haendlerbund.de/themen/unternehmen-maerkte/1746-gesucht-sind-innovative-und-mittelstandsgeeignete-it-loesungen)
- I found no independent confirmation of the "E-POST Premium Partner" claim. The pages of Deutsche Post E-POST Solutions that I found do not name easyNova or Siecom — [Deutsche Post E-POST Solutions](https://www.deutschepost.de/de/e/epost-solutions.html); [Deutsche Post references](https://www.deutschepost.de/de/e/epost-solutions/referenzen.html)
- Trustpilot: TrustScore 4.1, only 5 reviews (all 5 stars, all from February to June 2024, all very short and without concrete detail). No reviews in the last 12 months. The profile has been claimed by the company since January 2024, and there are no company replies — [Trustpilot easynova.de](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/easynova.de)
- Vendor claim (displayed on its own website): Google rating 5.0 from 7 reviews — [easyNova Startseite](https://www.easynova.de/)
- A search for "easyNova digiBrief Erfahrungen" found no independent experience reports, test reports or press articles. Most hits were for competitors (eBrief, Post AT/CH) — [search result example: ebrief.de](https://www.ebrief.de/en/EU)
- Siecom's parent website lists easyNova as its own product (IP telephony "easyNova®" and "Briefe online versenden" from 0,65 €) and links to an onboarding form for "easyNova digiBrief" customers — [siecom.de](https://www.siecom.de/)

### Inferences
- The very small number of reviews (5 Trustpilot reviews, all concentrated in 2024 and with no content) carries almost no evidential weight. It neither proves nor rules out good quality. The near-simultaneous, content-free 5-star reviews right after the profile was claimed fit a typical review-invitation campaign. This is an assessment, not a finding.
- The "BEST OF" award from the Initiative Mittelstand is widespread marketing and not a quality seal. It says little about reliability.
- Certificates (ISO, DICIS) and the BNetzA licence can be checked, but I did not verify them. If in doubt, the operator could ask DICIS AG to confirm the certificate number and ask the BNetzA Anbieterverzeichnis (anbieterverzeichnis-post@bnetza.de) about the entry. The operator must decide whether to do this, because the task was "do not contact the company", and asking third parties is something else.
- The decisive practical proof for the operator is not in public sources: whether the 58 letters actually arrived. Examples are a test letter to the operator's own address, or recipients who scanned the QR code. Scan events in the operator's own system would be strong evidence of real delivery.

### Gaps
- I could not open archive.org (fetch blocked, curl connection reset). So there is no independent proof of how old the digiBrief product is. DENIC RDAP shows only "last changed 2022-10-24" and nameservers at SchlundTech (IONOS group), with no creation date — [DENIC RDAP easynova.de](https://rdap.denic.de/domain/easynova.de)
- Not found: press articles (Sächsische Zeitung, DNN), IHK Dresden mentions, trade-fair presence, entries on Capterra, OMR Reviews or GetApp, and DATEV or ERP marketplace listings for easyNova.
- Not checked: the DPMA register for the trademark "easyNova®" (the vendor claims it is a registered trademark of Siecom — [Impressum](https://www.easynova.de/impressum)). This can be checked at [DPMAregister](https://register.dpma.de).

## 3. Does Gutzkowstraße 30, Dresden host a real business? Other companies at the same address?

Phone and business directories list Siecom and easyNova at this address, and another, unrelated company (ScIDre Scientific Instruments Dresden GmbH) is also based there. This fits a normal office building, not a letterbox address.

- Das Örtliche lists "easyNova.de – Briefe online versenden, Software, Gutzkowstr. 30, 01069 Dresden". Siecom appears at the same address with phone 0351 86 67 55 00 — [dasoertliche.de Dresden (category listing)](https://www.dasoertliche.de/Themen/Computer--Systeme--Zubehör/Dresden-Seite-5.html) (seen only in a search snippet)

### Inferences
- The phone numbers 86675-600 (easyNova), 86675-555 (Siecom helpdesk, [siecom.de](https://www.siecom.de/)) and 86675-500 (directory) are extensions of one shared number block. That fits one company with several product lines.
- The address 29–33 for the student residence most likely covers only the odd numbers (one side of the street). The fact that ScIDre and Siecom both sit at no. 30 suggests a commercial building on the opposite side. I did not verify this with a site photo or map.
- Printing does not happen in Dresden. By the vendor's own account it is outsourced to print service providers. So the address is only the administrative and support location.

### Gaps
- There is no image or map confirmation of the building, and I did not check the full list of companies at the address (an implisense address page for Gutzkowstraße 30 was not found).

## 5. Connection to other brands (e.g. "easyNova Personalmanagement", payroll software) and what that implies

### Takeaway
"easyNova" is a Siecom brand for two product lines: the hybrid letter (digiBrief) and VoIP telephone systems. I found no evidence of a link to HR or payroll software under the same name. The parent company, Siecom, is a classic regional IT systems house (consulting, infrastructure, helpdesk, ERP/DMS/CRM resale). The letter service is therefore a side business of an established IT service provider.

### Cited Findings
- Impressum: "easyNova® ist eine eingetragene Marke der Siecom IT-Systemhaus GmbH", used for the hybrid letter platform digiBrief and VoIP telephone systems. No group or subsidiary companies are named. Linked pages include Briefe-online-versenden.de, Briefporto.online and an E-Post-Ratgeber, without any statement of who owns them — [easyNova Impressum](https://www.easynova.de/impressum)
- Siecom's services, per its own website: IT strategy, system integration, helpdesk, full IT operation, IP telephony "easyNova®", "Briefe online versenden", and reseller and partner products (Fujitsu, ESET, SelectLine, ELO, CAS genesisWorld, FTAPI). Siecom claims "Seit 1991" and "Über 30 Jahre Erfahrung" (vendor claim) — [siecom.de](https://www.siecom.de/)
- The registered business purpose covers hardware and software trading and rental, office equipment, consulting, software development, website creation, telecommunication services and advertising — [North Data](https://www.northdata.de/SIECOM+IT-Systemhaus+GmbH,+Dresden/Amtsgericht+Dresden+HRB+25046)
- A search for "easyNova Siecom Telefonanlage" found no independent source connecting the VoIP line with Siecom. The connection rests only on the Impressum and siecom.de — [search results incl. aroundhome](https://www.aroundhome.de/fachfirmen/firma/siecom-it-systemhaus-gmbh/)
- I found no "easyNova Personalmanagement" or payroll product. Search hits for "easy … HR" belong to the unrelated easy software AG (easy HR) — [easy-software.com](https://easy-software.com/en/produkt/easy-hr-en/page/2/)

### Inferences
- The structure (an established local IT systems house that also runs a hybrid-mail portal and outsources printing to print service providers) is plausible and common in the industry. The company itself writes that it offers white label, so easyNova may itself be a reseller or platform operator.
- For the operator, this means a real legal entity with a long history and liable assets stands behind the service. If there is a dispute (for example, letters billed but not delivered), there is an identifiable, registered contractual partner with a managing director who has been the same since 2006.

### Gaps
- It is unclear which print service provider or postal partner actually printed and delivered the letters (Deutsche Post vs. private postal provider). This is not publicly documented and is relevant to the question of delivery.
- I found no evidence for or against a separate brand "easyNova Personalmanagement". If the operator has seen such a brand, it is probably a different company with a similar name.

## Overall assessment (for the report writer)
- **Verified (registry or third-party sources):** The GmbH has existed since 2006 under HRB 25046 at AG Dresden. Annual accounts were filed without gaps up to FY2024 (published 05/2026). There are no visible insolvency or liquidation notices. The managing director has apparently been the same since 2006. The address is entered in the register and in several directories, with other firms in the same building. The Impressum matches the register.
- **Vendor claims only (not verified):** hybrid mail since 2016, former E-POST Premium Partner, BNetzA licence L 4436, ISO 9001/14001/27001 (DICIS), printing only in Germany, delivery by Deutsche Post.
- **Weak spots:** almost no independent reviews or press, only 5 content-free Trustpilot reviews, and the "BEST OF" award is a marketing seal.
- **Conclusion:** The public evidence points to a real, small, long-established business and not to a scam company. Whether the 58 specific letters were actually printed and delivered cannot be answered from company data. It can only be answered through the operator's own delivery evidence (test letter to own address, QR scans, returned mail) or through the dispatch proofs provided by easyNova.
